# Item-writing rules (grounding) — every reading MCQ must satisfy these

These rules exist because the single biggest failure mode of generated items is an item with zero or
two defensible keys, or distractors a test-wise candidate eliminates without reading. An item that
breaks any rule below is invalid regardless of how good the passage is.

## The key
1. Exactly ONE option is defensibly correct given only the passage. No second option can be argued
   from the text.
2. The key is derivable from the passage alone — never from outside knowledge the candidate may or
   may not have.
3. The key is NOT a verbatim sentence lifted from the passage. At Level 3+ it paraphrases, infers,
   or synthesises, so pattern-matching the wording does not reveal it.
4. The correct option is not systematically the longest, the most detailed, the most hedged, or the
   most "textbook-correct"-sounding. Keep all four options parallel in length, grammar, and specificity.
5. Vary the key position across items (do not cluster on A or C). Target a roughly even A/B/C/D spread
   across a section.

## The distractors
6. Each distractor is wrong, but wrong for a reason a real candidate would fall for: a misread of a
   specific sentence, an over-generalisation, a reversed cause/effect or comparison, a true statement
   that does not answer the question, or a plausible outside assumption the text does not support.
7. No distractor is absurd, off-topic, or eliminable on grammar or length alone.
8. No "All of the above" / "None of the above" / "Both A and B" options.
9. Avoid absolute-word tells ("always", "never", "only") that flag a distractor as false by reflex,
   unless the passage itself warrants the absolute.

## The question
10. The stem tests comprehension at the target level (inference, stance, tone, function, main
    argument, vocabulary-in-context for Level 3), not trivia or memory of an isolated number.
11. The stem is answerable from the passage without the options visible (it is not the options that
    make it answerable).
12. One clear task per item. No double-barrelled questions.

## Output contract
- Provide a rationale for EVERY option: why the key is correct, and the specific misread each
  distractor targets. The rationale is used by the blind-solver verifier and by the answer key.
- Topics must be authentic and analytical (see topic-domains.md). No childish or contrived scenarios
  at Level 3+.
- Text must be original. Do not reproduce real exam passages or copyrighted articles verbatim.
