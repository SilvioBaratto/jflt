# Authentic topic domains (grounding)

STANAG Level 3 / C1 reading uses the register of quality journalism and policy analysis. Draw topics
from these domains and write in an analytical, impersonal register — the voice of an editorial, a
policy brief, or in-depth reporting. Avoid casual blog tone and avoid military jargon the candidate
would not meet in open-source reading.

- **Geopolitics & international relations** — alliances, treaties, deterrence, sanctions, border
  disputes, multilateral institutions, spheres of influence.
- **Security & defence policy** — defence budgets, procurement debates, conscription vs professional
  forces, hybrid and cyber threats, peacekeeping, civil-military relations (open-source framing).
- **Economics** — trade policy, energy markets, inflation, supply chains, development, labour markets.
- **Science & technology** — AI governance, climate science, space, biotech, dual-use technology.
- **Society & culture** — migration, demographics, media and disinformation, public health, education.
- **Environment** — climate policy, resource competition, energy transition, disaster response.

For each item, give the passage a specific angle (a claim, a tension, a trade-off, or a shift) rather
than a flat encyclopaedic summary, so the question can test argument and stance, not just facts.
