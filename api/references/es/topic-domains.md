# Authentic topic domains (grounding, Spanish)

STANAG Level 3 / C1 reading uses the register of quality journalism and policy analysis. For the
Spanish set the models are Spanish-language: analysis and commentary from think tanks such as the Real
Instituto Elcano, international-affairs reviews such as *Política Exterior*, academic outreach such as
the Spanish edition of *The Conversation*, and the language of official documents such as Spain's
*Estrategia de Seguridad Nacional*. They are models of register and angle only: nothing is copied,
translated or closely paraphrased from them. Draw topics from the domains below and write in an
analytical, impersonal register — the voice of an editorial, a policy brief, or in-depth reporting.
Avoid casual blog tone, and avoid military jargon or untranslated English terms the candidate would
not meet in open-source Spanish reading.

- **Geopolitics & international relations** — alliances, treaties, deterrence, sanctions, border
  disputes, multilateral institutions, spheres of influence. Natural Spanish-language vantage points:
  NATO's southern flank, the Mediterranean and the Sahel, EU–Latin America relations, European
  strategic autonomy (*autonomía estratégica*).
- **Security & defence policy** — defence budgets, procurement debates, conscription vs professional
  forces, hybrid and cyber threats, disinformation, foreign interference, peacekeeping, civil-military
  relations (open-source framing). Use the terms of Spanish official texts: *estrategias híbridas*,
  *campañas de desinformación*, *injerencias desde el exterior*, *vulnerabilidad del ciberespacio*.
- **Economics** — trade policy, energy markets and energy vulnerability (*vulnerabilidad energética*),
  inflation, supply chains, development, labour markets.
- **Science & technology** — AI governance, climate science, space, biotech, dual-use technology,
  technological sovereignty (*soberanía tecnológica*).
- **Society & culture** — migration (*flujos migratorios*), demographics (ageing, rural depopulation),
  media and disinformation, public health, education.
- **Environment** — climate policy, resource competition (water, drought), energy transition,
  emergencies and disaster response (*emergencias y catástrofes*).

For each item, give the passage a specific angle (a claim, a tension, a trade-off, or a shift) rather
than a flat encyclopaedic summary, so the question can test argument and stance, not just facts.
Spanish analysis typically frames its angle as a paradox (doing one thing while needing its opposite),
a gap between ambition and capacity, a question left open, or a metaphor of contest. The item heading
`### N. (topic)` states the angle in Spanish as a claim, in sentence case — e.g. *(La autonomía
estratégica europea depende de proveedores que no controla)*, not a domain label such as *(Defensa
europea)*.

- **Spread.** In each 30-item level cover all six domains, about five items each, and give no two
  items the same angle.
- **Durability and neutrality.** Prefer angles that will not date quickly and do not hinge on a named
  current politician or a recent event the candidate may know from news in their own language.
  Invented but plausible specifics are fine (*un Estado miembro del flanco oriental*, *el
  ministerio*). Quote no real person, and keep content free of anything offensive about age, gender,
  ethnicity or religion.
- **Spanish setting, no Spanish trivia.** A passage may be set in Spain, Europe or Latin America, but
  the answer must not depend on knowing Spanish politics or institutions beyond what the text states
  (item-writing rule 2).

## Fuentes
- *Real Decreto 1150/2021, por el que se aprueba la Estrategia de Seguridad Nacional 2021*
  (BOE-A-2021-21884), copy hosted by ENISA —
  https://www.enisa.europa.eu/sites/default/files/ncss-map/strategies/additional-documents/ES_National%20Security%20Strategy%202021_es.pdf
  — Spanish official terms for risks and threats (*estrategias híbridas*, *campañas de
  desinformación*, *injerencias desde el exterior*, *vulnerabilidad energética*, *flujos migratorios
  irregulares*, *emergencias y catástrofes*) and *autonomía estratégica*.
- Real Instituto Elcano, home page and research areas — https://www.realinstitutoelcano.org/ —
  publication types (análisis, comentarios, policy papers), topic areas, headline framing.
- Real Instituto Elcano, P. Oliver Llorente, «La soberanía tecnológica de la UE tiene una pregunta sin
  responder: ¿para qué la queremos?» (19 October 2026) —
  https://www.realinstitutoelcano.org/analisis/la-soberania-tecnologica-de-la-ue-tiene-una-pregunta-sin-responder-para-que-la-queremos/
  — an analysis built on an open question and a concession-then-pivot; register model only.
- *Política Exterior*, home page — https://www.politicaexterior.com/ — headline framing (contest
  metaphors, ambition-versus-capacity gaps, open questions).
- *The Conversation* (Spanish edition), home page — https://theconversation.com/es — science,
  technology, environment and society topics written by academics for general readers.
- BILC, *Item Review Checklist* (Herzog and Seinhorst, 2014) —
  https://nato-bilc.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Item-Review-Checklist.pdf — texts that do not date
  quickly, no prior knowledge from native-language sources, no sensitive or offensive content.
- NATO, *ATrainP-5 Language Proficiency Levels*, Edition A, Version 2 (May 2016) —
  https://nato-bilc.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/ATrainP-5-EDA-V2-E.pdf — Level 3 reading contexts
  (news, informational and editorial items; economics, culture, science, technology).
